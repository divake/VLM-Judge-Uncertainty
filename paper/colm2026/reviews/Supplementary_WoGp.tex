\section*{Boundary-adjustment implementation (R2)}

For completeness, the boundary-adjustment routine applies the expansion form
$[l,u]\mapsto[\lfloor l\rfloor,\lceil u\rceil]$, not the shrinkage form printed in
Sec. 3.3. This is consistent with the expansion branch of the cited Theorem 1
(Sheng et al., 2025) and with the reported increase in coverage and width after
adjustment (LLaVA-Critic R2CCP: $0.900/3.05 \to 0.981/3.60$).

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
def boundary_adjust_expand(lows, highs):
    # Expand: floor lower, ceil upper
    return np.clip(np.floor(lows), 1, 5), np.clip(np.ceil(highs), 1, 5)
\end{lstlisting}
